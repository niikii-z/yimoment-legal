Privacy Policy

Yi Moment · 一念 — Last updated: October 2026

Yi Moment · 一念 (“the app”) is provided by Ngar Lab (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. 繁體中文版本在下方。

What we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider (Supabase); we cannot read them.
  • Usage balance — how many in-depth interpretations you have used and have left, and your subscription status.
  • Purchase information — purchases are processed by Apple. Our purchase provider (RevenueCat) receives your anonymous account ID and purchase receipts so we can unlock what you bought. We never see your payment card or Apple ID details.
  • Questions you ask for an in-depth interpretation — when you request an interpretation, the question you typed, your hexagram and the app language are sent to our server and on to an AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the text. We do not store the question on our servers.

What stays on your device

Your readings, journal entries, questions and saved interpretations are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, or use “Clear all readings” in Settings, they are gone and we cannot recover them.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — accounts, sign-in and usage balances.
  • Anthropic — generates in-depth interpretations from the question and hexagram you send.
  • RevenueCat and Apple — in-app purchases and subscriptions.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, remaining interpretations and subscription record from our servers. It does not cancel an App Store subscription — you must cancel that in your Apple ID settings. You can also email us and we will delete your account for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Not professional advice

Readings and interpretations are for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do. Questions: ngar.universe@gmail.com

私隱政策

一念 Yi Moment — 最後更新：2026 年 10 月

「一念 Yi Moment」（下稱「本 App」）由 Ngar Lab（下稱「我們」）提供。本政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資料及原因。

我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料 — 你建立帳號時提供的姓名、電郵及密碼。密碼由我們的驗證服務供應商（Supabase）以加密方式儲存，我們無法讀取。
  • 使用額度 — 你已使用及剩餘的深度解讀次數，以及訂閱狀態。
  • 購買資料 — 購買由 Apple 處理。我們的購買服務供應商（RevenueCat）會收到你的匿名帳號 ID 及購買憑證，以便解鎖你購買的內容。我們不會看到你的付款卡或 Apple ID 資料。
  • 你為深度解讀提出的問題 — 當你要求深度解讀時，你輸入的問題、卦象及 App 語言會傳送至我們的伺服器，再傳送給 AI 服務供應商（Anthropic）以生成內容。我們不會在伺服器上儲存你的問題。

只保存在你裝置上的資料

你的占卜紀錄、日誌、問題及已儲存的解讀只儲存在你的裝置上。刪除本 App，或在「設定」使用「清除所有占卜紀錄」後，資料即會消失，我們無法復原。

我們不會做的事

  • 不顯示廣告。
  • 不會跨 App 或網站追蹤你。
  • 不會出售你的資料。
  • 不使用第三方分析或廣告 SDK。

協助我們處理資料的供應商

  • Supabase — 帳號、登入及使用額度。
  • Anthropic — 根據你傳送的問題及卦象生成深度解讀。
  • RevenueCat 與 Apple — 應用內購買及訂閱。

刪除你的資料

你可隨時在 App 內刪除帳號：設定 → 刪除帳號。這會從我們的伺服器永久移除你的帳號、剩餘解讀次數及訂閱紀錄。刪除帳號不會取消 App Store 的訂閱，請到 Apple ID 設定自行取消。你亦可電郵聯絡我們，由我們代為刪除。

兒童

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童，我們亦不會有意收集其個人資料。

並非專業意見

占卜結果及解讀只供反思與娛樂，並非醫療、法律、財務或其他專業意見。

更新與聯絡

我們可能更新本政策，屆時上方日期會更改。如有查詢：ngar.universe@gmail.com

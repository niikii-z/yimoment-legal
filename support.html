Common questions 常見問題

I bought interpretations but they didn’t appear.
Open the plan screen (Settings → your interpretation balance) and tap “Restore purchase”. If it still doesn’t show, email us. 請到「設定」→ 深度解讀 → 點「還原購買項目」，若仍未出現請電郵聯絡我們。

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Yi Moment. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it. 請到 iPhone「設定」→ 你的名字 → 訂閱 → 一念。刪除 App 或帳號不會取消訂閱。

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete account. 在 App 內：設定 → 刪除帳號。

Where are my readings stored?
Only on your device. 只儲存在你的裝置上。
